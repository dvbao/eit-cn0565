Table 1 shows the facial gesture classification result summary. The
classification results indicate strong overall performance. The model
achieved an average accuracy of 93%, demonstrating its reliability
across subjects. The macro recall (91%) suggests that the model
performs well in identifying all classes, though subject 2 had a
relatively lower recall (83%), indicating some misclassification. The
macro F1-score (92%) reflects a good balance between precision and
recall, confirming the model’s robustness. Overall, the classifier
shows strong and consistent performance across different subjects,
with slight variability in recall.

5     Conclusion
In this study, we showcased the promising potential of bio-impedance
sensing for facial gesture recognition. Using a two-channel bio-
impedance signal collected from the face, we successfully identified
four distinct cheek movements and null class with a random forest
classifier, achieving an average accuracy of 93%. This high accuracy
